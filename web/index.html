        Insights

        A spreadsheet file with one row per post. Instagram forgets its numbers after about 90 days; the app keeps yours.

        What the numbers mean
        Reach
        How many different people saw a post.
        Views
        How many times it was played or shown. One person can count several times.
        Engagement rate
        Likes, comments, saves and shares together, as a share of the people reached.
        Saves and shares
        The strongest signs a quote landed: someone kept it, or sent it to someone.
        Average watch time
        How long a reel was watched per play, on average.
        Left in the first 3 seconds
        The share of first views that swiped away almost at once.
